Our Story

Hively Farms is built on family, tradition, and a strong connection to the land. What started as a family operation continues today with the same values: hard work, responsibility, and pride in raising quality livestock.

Our goal is to honor the generations before us while building something lasting for the next generation.

History

The history of Hively Farms is rooted in rural life, livestock care, and the everyday work that keeps a family farm moving forward. Over the years, the farm has seen changes in buildings, equipment, animals, and goals, but the commitment to doing things the right way has remained the same.
